Opportunities we identified

  • High-ticket lead follow-up and price transparency. Four MahaRERA-registered Mumbai launches priced Rs 1.73-6.91 crore, with enquiries arriving via WhatsApp Business, toll-free callbacks and forms while reviews cite pricing and transparency concerns.[1][5]

What we recommend

  1. Mumbai Enquiry Concierge (Adani Verve, Linkbay Residences Phase II Wing B, Reform Heights and Adani Triumph; top of funnel to site-visit booking.). Retrieval-only WhatsApp and callback assistant that answers price band, configuration, availability and MahaRERA number from approved documents, scores leads and writes follow-up tasks to CRM.
