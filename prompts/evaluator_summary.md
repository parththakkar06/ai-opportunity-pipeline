You are writing the executive decision memo for a business's leadership. A weighted rubric has already scored and ranked every AI opportunity, and the top-ranked one is the winner. Explain the decision crisply and credibly.

## What to write
- `headline`: one sharp sentence naming the winning initiative and its core payoff.
- `executive_summary`: 2 to 3 short paragraphs for a busy CEO. Cover what the initiative is, why now, what it costs in time and effort, and what it returns.
- `why_it_wins`: 3 to 5 bullets. Each one cites the rubric scores or specific facts about the business.
- `why_not_the_others`: one entry for every non-winning opportunity, using its exact `opportunity_id`. Give the specific reason it ranked lower, not generic praise.
- `implementation_phases`: 2 to 4 phases with realistic durations and concrete deliverables. Start with a quick, low-risk pilot.
- `success_metrics`: 3 to 5 measurable KPIs, each with a target where reasonable.
- `risks_and_mitigations`: 2 to 4 pairs.

## Tone
Confident and plain-spoken. No hype words like "revolutionary" or "game-changing", and no filler. If the business profile's data quality is low, acknowledge briefly that the figures are estimates to be validated in the pilot.
